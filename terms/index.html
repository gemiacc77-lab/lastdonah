OPTILINE provides digital growth assets, including but not limited to brand identity, social media content, and paid advertising creatives. The specific scope of work is defined strictly by the Package you select and the data you submit in your Project Brief.

  • Any feature, design, or service not explicitly selected or described in the Project Brief is considered out of scope.
  • Out-of-scope requests will require a separate agreement and additional billing.

03. Revisions & Approvals

We work systematically through Milestone Deliveries to ensure total alignment with your vision. Our revision policy is designed to protect both the project timeline and the quality of the final output.

  • Definition of a Revision: A revision is a minor modification or adjustment to the presented concept (e.g., color tweaks, font changes, layout adjustments).
  • Redesigns are not Revisions: A request to completely change the artistic direction after initial approval, or contradicting the original Project Brief, constitutes a "Redesign" and will be billed separately.
  • Once a milestone is explicitly approved by the client, any subsequent changes to that specific milestone will incur additional hourly rates.

In the rare event a refund is mutually agreed upon before work begins, it will be processed manually in USDT at the exact USD equivalent value of the original transaction, regardless of market fluctuations.
